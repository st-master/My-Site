🎬 うめきた巨大絵ワークショップ：Cityレトリート動画プロンプト制作

うめきたの「グラングリーン大阪」での体験型アート展（近藤さやか氏・巨大絵ワークショップ）やカフェ「LOHE」、セイレーン姫の世界観を融合した「Cityレトリート動画」用の画像生成プロンプトを作成しました。

カット1：現実と異次元の境界（うめきた未来都市とポータル）
Cinematic wide angle shot of a futuristic Umekita cityscape with modern glass skyscrapers and lush green park in Osaka. In front of a stylish Starbucks cafe, a glowing, mystical glowing portal of emerald green and turquoise swirling energy emerges in the air, creating a portal to an otherworldly realm. City retreat vibe, hyper-realistic, photorealistic, 8k resolution, volumetric lighting, epic dramatic mood.
カット2：ワークショップとセイレーン姫（現実と夢の融合）
Inside a futuristic art studio cafe in Umekita, children in green aprons are gathered around a table, painting a giant puzzle canvas with vibrant colors. Beside them stands a beautiful, ethereal Siren Princess with shimmering scales, glowing aquamarine hair, and a regal crown, gently guiding their hands with a glowing paintbrush. Magical sparkling dust and holographic light floating in the air. Dreamy, magical realism, cinematic lighting, ultra-detailed, 8k.
カット3：完成した巨大絵と深海未来都市（カフェに広がる深海の世界）
Interior view of a quiet, modern coffee lounge in Umekita. On the main wall hangs a massive, breathtaking glowing mural painting depicting a futuristic underwater city where futuristic sleek submarines and sea creatures swim together around submerged skyscrapers. Soft cinematic lighting, peaceful city retreat atmosphere, deeply immersive, 8k, photorealistic, masterpiece.
